Kukku 2.0 (Build 14) - Major Update

A complete redesign that turns Kukku into a daily companion, plus four new agents, per-agent Journals, and native skills — now with powerful on-device AI powered by the Gemma 4 model.

New

  • Redesigned experience: refreshed UI, new animations, and emoji icons rendered with Metal shaders.
  • Home Dashboard: Kukku Smart card and Reflection card, CTA agents, and horizontal rails; All Agents view with grid and card layouts.
  • Four new seeded agents: Habits, Expenses, Food & Nutrition (Nutrition Analyst) and Everyday Agent.
  • Journals: a per-agent feature with today, weekly and monthly views for habits, expenses, nutrition and health (analytics runs internally).
  • Skills: Kukku native Habit Tracker and Expense Tracker skills, plus support for additional skills following the skills.md standard (skills and tools are mutually exclusive per agent).
  • Camera capture: snap a receipt to log an expense, or a meal to estimate calories and macros.
  • Gemma 4 model: a stronger local model option on capable devices.
  • Escalation & remote models: connect an agent to a remote or OpenAI-compatible endpoint with your own API key.
  • Experimental MCP tools: early Model Context Protocol support.

Improved

  • Updated Health & Fitness agent, Image Analyzer and Knowledge Base.
  • Redesigned agent chat with smoother animations.
  • Refreshed Nearby Agents and OpenClaw-compatible bot bridge.

Release status: Kukku 2.0 (Build 14) is Apple approved and set to release on August 17, 2026, bringing powerful on-device AI powered by the Gemma 4 model. Feature availability can vary by device memory; calorie, macro and health figures are estimates and not medical advice.
