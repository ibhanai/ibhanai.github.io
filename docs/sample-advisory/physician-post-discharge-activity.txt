IBHANAI SAMPLE ADVISORY — PHYSICIAN POST-DISCHARGE ACTIVITY NOTE
==============================================================
Purpose: Upload this file to a IbhanAi agent's knowledge base (for example a
Health & Fitness agent) so activity suggestions respect post-discharge limits.
Sample content — edit or replace with your own physician's written note.

PATIENT CONTEXT
- Situation: recovering at home after a minor procedure. Cleared for light
  activity only, with a graded return over two weeks.
- Limits provided by the physician (the agent must respect these):
  * Weeks 1-2: short, gentle walks only. No lifting over 5 kg.
  * No high-intensity exercise, running, or core strain until reviewed.
  * Stop and rest on any pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.

PHYSICIAN'S GUIDANCE (the agent should follow this)
1. Keep all activity suggestions within the limits above. When unsure, suggest
   the more conservative option.
2. Encourage gradual, gentle progression (e.g. add 2-3 minutes of walking every
   few days if comfortable).
3. Emphasise rest, hydration, and sleep as part of recovery.
4. For anything outside gentle activity, advise the user to confirm with their
   physician before proceeding.

SAFETY ESCALATION (important)
If the user reports warning signs — chest pain, severe or worsening pain,
fever, breathlessness, fainting — the agent must NOT give exercise advice and
should tell the user to contact their doctor or emergency services.

TONE
Calm, cautious, reassuring. Prioritise safety over progress.

DISCLAIMER
Sample content for demonstrating knowledge-base grounding in IbhanAi. This is
NOT medical advice and is not a real physician's instruction. Always follow the
written discharge instructions from your own treating clinician.
